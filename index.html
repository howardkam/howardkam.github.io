Professional Background & Philosophy

Connecting Real-World Engineering Practices with High School Learners

With over two decades in the technology sector and more than 12 years in secondary education, my mission at Roosevelt High School is to prepare Hawaii’s youth for the evolving digital economy.

Rather than teaching abstract syntax in isolation, my curriculum emphasizes computational thinking, open-source practices, version control workflows, and creative computing. Students build authentic, portfolio-ready projects ranging from responsive web applications to interactive 2D/3D games.
