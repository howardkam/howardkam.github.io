GRADUATION HONOR DIPLOMA

CTE Honors Recognition Criteria

To qualify to wear the official CTE Honors Stole during the Roosevelt High School graduation ceremony in the Information Technology Pathway, students must meet all of the following requirements:

01
Cumulative GPA: Minimum 3.0 cumulative overall GPA.
02
Pathway Sequence: Complete a sequential 2–3 course program starting with Foundations (TIF1000).
03
Course Marks: Earn a "B" or better (3.0 GPA) in each required pathway course.
04
Performance-Based Assessment: Meet or exceed proficiency on:
  • Technical writing documentation
  • Deliverable internship or project design
  • Formal project presentation
